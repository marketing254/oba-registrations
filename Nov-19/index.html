About this session

Educate patients well without turning the visit into a pitch

Join the Ophthalmology Business Academy for a focused discussion on building a trusted premium cataract counseling system without turning education into a sales conversation. The panel will explore how practices discuss elective and premium options in a patient-centred way, which education and expectation-setting steps should happen before lens options, how to keep counseling consistent across the team without feeling scripted, and what expectation mismatches teach about selection, counseling quality, and success measurement.

This discussion will examine how to support informed patient choice without the conversation feeling commercially driven; which lifestyle assessment, expectation-setting, and education steps belong before specific lenses are discussed, and who should lead them; how surgeons, counselors, technicians, and digital education can deliver one consistent process; and what dissatisfied patients and declined recommendations reveal about patient selection and how premium cataract success should really be measured.

This panel is for you if…

  • You own, lead, or manage a cataract or refractive practice
  • Your premium conversion is inconsistent from one surgeon or counselor to the next
  • You want patients educated before lens options come up, not sold to after
  • Your team's counseling feels scripted, or patients say it feels like a pitch
  • You've had dissatisfied premium patients and want to know what the process missed
